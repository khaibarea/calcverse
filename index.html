About Us

About CalcVerse

CalcVerse is a modern, beautiful, and free web-based unit converter and calculator. Our goal is to provide everyone with simple, fast, and accurate conversion services.

We believe tools should be free, ad-free, and privacy-first. That's why all calculations in CalcVerse run in your browser — no data is ever sent to a server.

  • ✔ 100% free, no sign-up required
  • ✔ Open-source friendly architecture
  • ✔ GitHub auto-deployment support
  • ✔ Fast loading & animated UI
